Privacy Policy

Block Puzzle Daily (launcher name: Daily Block)

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Block Puzzle Daily (“we,” “us,” or “the App”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Android application for casual block puzzle gameplay (Classic, Daily Challenge, and Adventure modes).

Product summary: Block Puzzle Daily is free forever, contains no advertising, and offers no real-money purchases. Core gameplay works offline. Soft currency (coins / gems), boosters, themes, and skins are earned or unlocked through play and are stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information Collected by the App Itself

Block Puzzle Daily is an offline-first puzzle game. It does not require you to register or sign in, and it does not upload your gameplay boards, screenshots, or full progress history to our own servers. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, nickname, country/region selection, postal address, government-issued identification number, or similar information for gameplay;
  • We do not actively read or upload device identifiers that can directly identify an individual, such as IMEI, device serial number, ANDROID_ID, Advertising ID (GAID), or Wi-Fi MAC address, for our own server-side storage;
  • Language preference, sound / music / haptic / graphics settings, cosmetic avatar choice, and similar preferences are stored locally on your device (app storage / WebView local storage);
  • Scores, statistics, daily challenge calendar and streaks, adventure progress, missions, achievements, inventory (coins, gems, boosters), unlocked themes / skins / looks, and in-progress game saves are stored locally on your device;
  • Optional score / result share cards are generated on your device. If you choose Share, the Android system share sheet (or browser share API) lets you send text and/or an image to an app you select. Sharing happens only when you actively initiate it;
  • The app may use vibration / haptic feedback when enabled in settings. This processing stays on your device;
  • Crash diagnostics may be sent to Firebase Crashlytics as described in Section 1.2. Separately, you may copy or share technical information through the system share sheet if you choose to do so.

[Important] About your game progress: Progress and preferences are stored on your device. We do not operate a cloud save or account sync service. Uninstalling the app, clearing app data, or using “Reset All Progress” in Settings will remove local app data according to system / app rules.

No social / cloud services: The app does not provide online multiplayer, social feeds, cloud media hosting, or user account services.

1.2 Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect certain information when network access is available:

Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the app and to improve the experience. Firebase Analytics may collect:

  • App usage events: screen or feature usage, flow statistics, and similar data;
  • Device information: device model, Android version, language settings, screen-related parameters, and similar data;
  • Performance and stability metrics: launch and runtime statistics (as actually provided by Google).

Important: Firebase Analytics is used for product analytics so we can understand how the app is used and improve the experience. It is not used to upload your full game boards, personal messages, or media files. For more information, see Google’s official documentation: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to automatically detect and report app crashes and errors so we can fix issues and improve stability. Crashlytics may collect:

  • Device information: device model, Android version, manufacturer, and similar data;
  • App state: information related to app operation at the time of a crash;
  • Error information: stack traces, error messages, crash logs, and other technical diagnostic information.

Important: Crashlytics is used for technical diagnostics and app improvement. Crash reports may include technical context visible in a stack trace, but are not intended to include the content of your personal files. For more information, see Google’s official documentation: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Firebase-related data is handled in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy. For more information, see: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Firebase data note: Data collected through Firebase may include device and app technical information, usage events, app-instance identifiers, and crash diagnostics as described above. Depending on the SDK configuration and device settings, Firebase Analytics may also collect the Android Advertising ID (GAID) where available. This data is processed by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy and applicable Firebase terms. We do not use Firebase to operate a user account system or to store your game progress on our own servers, and we do not use Firebase data to display advertisements in this app.

Important: We do not provide a user account system and do not operate our own servers for storing users’ personal data or game progress. Data processed by Firebase is subject to Google’s data retention and security practices; retention periods follow Google’s rules.

No advertising SDKs: This app does not integrate AdMob or other advertising networks. Soft currency and cosmetics are not sold for real money. Some Google Play services / Firebase-related permissions listed below (including AD_ID and Ads Services-related permissions) may still appear in the merged Android manifest because of Google’s libraries. Their presence does not mean this app shows ads. Our Google Play Console Advertising ID declaration is intended to match the app’s actual Firebase configuration.

2. Permissions Requested by the App

The permissions below are based on the app’s Android manifest and the Release merged manifest for Block Puzzle Daily (com.dodo.blockpuzzle). The actual permissions shown on your device may differ by Android version; please refer to App info in your system settings.

2.1 Permissions Declared by This App

The following permissions are declared in our app manifest for core functionality:

Permission Purpose
android.permission.INTERNET Transmit Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data when a network connection is available. Core puzzle gameplay does not require the Internet.
android.permission.VIBRATE Provide optional haptic / vibration feedback during gameplay when you enable haptic feedback in Settings.

2.2 Permissions Merged Automatically by Third-Party SDKs

The following permissions may appear in the final merged app manifest because third-party SDKs (Firebase, Google Play services, and related libraries) automatically introduce them during the build process. They are not declared by our app code for active puzzle features such as camera, contacts, microphone, or precise location.

Permission Purpose
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE May be used by Google / Firebase libraries to check whether a network connection is available before sending analytics or crash reports.
android.permission.WAKE_LOCK May briefly hold a partial wake lock so background measurement or transmission tasks can complete.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID This permission may be automatically merged by Google Play services or Firebase-related components to support Google-provided measurement or attribution features, where applicable. This app does not show ads. The presence of this permission does not mean the app contains advertising.
android.permission.ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION This permission may be automatically declared by Google Play services-related components and relates to the Android Ads Services framework. Our app code does not directly call this API for advertising. The presence of this permission does not mean the app contains advertising.
android.permission.ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID This permission may be automatically declared by Google Play services-related components and relates to the Android Ads Services framework. Our app code does not directly call this API for advertising. The presence of this permission does not mean the app contains advertising.
com.google.android.finsky.permission.BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE This permission may be automatically declared by Google Play services or measurement components to obtain app install source information, where applicable.

[Access note] Block Puzzle Daily does not request camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, phone state, or storage / media-library permissions for core features. A FileProvider may be used only to help share a locally generated score card when you choose Share.

[Note] You can manage or revoke permissions in system settings. Revoking network access will not block offline play, but may prevent Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics from sending diagnostics. Disabling vibration / related system controls may limit haptic feedback.

3. System Sharing and Other Services

When you use Share Card (or similar share actions), the app uses Android / WebView share capabilities so you can choose the destination app. This flow involves the Android system and the app you choose. We only pass the content you choose to share when you actively initiate sharing, and we do not assume separate privacy obligations for how third-party receiving apps handle that data.

The app does not provide online social feeds, cloud progress sync, or user account services. If a future version adds new online features, we will update this policy and adjust permission and data security disclosures as needed.

4. How We Use Information

We use relevant information only for the following purposes:

  • Providing offline puzzle gameplay (Classic, Daily Challenge, Adventure) and related local features such as missions, achievements, boosters, and cosmetics;
  • Remembering your preferences (language, sound, haptics, graphics, layout options) and progress on your device;
  • Generating optional share cards locally when you choose to share a result;
  • Improving app performance and stability, and identifying and fixing crashes and errors;
  • Understanding app usage patterns to optimize the experience and user flows.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information for unrelated marketing. Data processing or disclosure may occur only in the following circumstances:

  • Firebase services: Technical and statistical data collected or processed by Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics is handled by Google in accordance with its Privacy Policy;
  • Your active sharing: Apps you choose through the system / browser share sheet;
  • Legal requirements: Where required by applicable law, or in response to lawful requests from courts or other competent authorities, we may disclose necessary information as required by law.

6. Data Security

We take your information security seriously. Gameplay, preferences, and most progress data are processed on your device. When analytics and crash diagnostics are transmitted through Firebase, industry-common transmission and protection measures are typically used (such as HTTPS, as actually implemented by Google). We do not sell your gameplay data for advertising. We do not transmit or store your game progress on our own servers.

Android backup: The app may participate in Android backup/restore mechanisms according to system and manifest settings (android:allowBackup). Backup behavior depends on your device, Android version, and Google account settings. Locally stored progress and preferences may be included in device backup if enabled by the system.
